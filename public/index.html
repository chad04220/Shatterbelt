PROCEDURAL ASTEROID BATTLE ROYALE
Survive a collapsing combat zone inside a dense asteroid belt. Every round, a modular ship factory chooses one of 12 distinct spacecraft skeletons and assembles it from hundreds of sleek low-poly hull, cockpit, armor, wing, engine, weapon, sensor, antenna and utility blueprints. Every installed component has independent hit points and collision, so crippled ships keep fighting until the cockpit or enough core structure is lost.
